# Payment brand artwork

Retrieved 2026-09-28. These are payment acceptance marks, not Bitir Gitsin brand assets. Keep the original colors, proportions, legibility and clear space. The `PaymentMethods` component provides a plain white background and does not make these marks into payment links.

## iyzico ile Öde

- Source: https://www.iyzico.com/hakkimizda/iyzico-marka-kilavuzu
- Official download: https://www.iyzico.com/assets/uploads/zip/BeyazZemindeYatayKullanimTR.zip
- File in archive: `iyzico-pay-horizontal-color-eng.svg` (despite its filename, the artwork is the Turkish **iyzico ile Öde** lockup).
- Stored unchanged as `iyzico-ile-ode.svg`. This is the horizontal version intended for white backgrounds.

## Visa

- Source: https://corporate.visa.com/en/about-visa/brand.html
- Download page: https://globalclient.visa.com/brand-mark-ty
- Official download: https://usa.visa.com/content/dam/VCOM/Brand/visa-brand-mark-digital-2025.zip
- File in archive: `Visa Brand Mark DIGITAL 2025/Visa Brand Mark DIGITAL/VBM_digital_2025/blue/VBM_bluRGB_2025.svg`.
- Stored unchanged as `visa.svg`, including its built-in clear space.
- Guidelines: https://corporate.visa.com/content/dam/VCOM/corporate/about-visa/documents/visa-brand-standards-sept2025.pdf
- Use Visa Blue on a light background, without distortion or recoloring. The mark is for approved merchants accepting Visa, subject to Visa's brand standards.

## Mastercard

- Source: iyzico's official merchant logo pack at https://docs.iyzico.com/ek-bilgiler/iyzico-logo-paketi
- File in archive: `iyzico-logo-pack/footer_iyzico_ile_ode/Colored/logo_band_colored.svg`.
- `mastercard.svg` extracts the Mastercard circles symbol from that payment acceptance strip. The three supplied symbol shapes, colors and proportions are preserved, with a fitting viewBox; no geometry was redrawn. The separate legacy wordmark is omitted to match the current Mastercard Symbol configuration for digital acceptance.
- Current brand requirements: https://www.mastercard.com/brandcenter/us/en/brand-requirements/mastercard.html
- Usage eligibility: https://www.mastercard.com/brandcenter/us/en/faq.html
- Mastercard allows accepting merchants to display the relevant acceptance marks. Retain full color, clear space, and prominence comparable to the other accepted payment marks.

All trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. Display these marks only for the payment methods offered by the service.
