# Kategori fotoğrafları

Ana sayfadaki sekiz kategori için yerleşimi koruyan, tutarlı sıcak ışık ve açık taş zemin kullanan yapay zekâ üretimi temsili fotoğraflar. Gerçek işletmelerin ürün fotoğrafları değildir.

Üretim: yerleşik imagegen aracı (builtin), 8 bağımsız görsel. Orijinal PNG dosyaları değiştirilmeden assets/images/categories/ içine kopyalandı. Kartlarda cihaz piksel oranına uygun decode boyutu kullanılır.

## restoran.png

Dosya: `bitir_yemek_mobile/assets/images/categories/restoran.png`

Üretim promptu:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: ONE square category thumbnail for a Turkish food marketplace mobile app, production asset, not a UI screenshot. Create an actual-looking professional food photograph, NOT an illustration, NOT a drawing, NOT a miniature shop, NOT 3D/CGI. Shared series art direction: warm ivory matte limestone tabletop fills the entire square, natural soft window light from upper left, gentle realistic contact shadows, premium editorial food photography, natural appetizing color, realistic edible textures. Slightly elevated 35-degree camera angle, 50 mm lens look, food entirely in crisp focus, clean uncluttered composition. Main subject arranged compactly in the center 75% of the square so it reads instantly in an 84-pixel thumbnail. Close enough for recognizable texture, very few objects, same visual scale and lighting across categories. Full-bleed opaque background, no people, no hands, no shop fronts, no lettering, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no border, no collage. Output one square photograph, ideally 1024 x 1024.
Subject: A shallow off-white ceramic plate of a freshly cooked Turkish restaurant meal: beautifully seared boneless chicken slices beside a small mound of rice pilaf, roasted tomato and grilled green pepper. A few fresh herbs, subtle real cooking marks. One coherent appetizing plate, food occupies most of the frame; no cutlery.
